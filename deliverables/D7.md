# D7 - Validation

## 1. Description

The NAU Lost and Found System is a web application that helps students, faculty, and staff on the NAU Flagstaff Mountain Campus recover lost belongings without the building-by-building guesswork of traditional lost and found desks. Its target users are NAU community members who have lost something on campus, together with the campus staff who receive and return found items. Its value proposition is that users file one report, and the system automatically matches it against items already turned in and shows the likely matches in their inbox, while the rest of the inventory remains private so that nobody can claim an item that isn't theirs.

The MVP covers the full path of a lost item. Community members report a lost item, choosing its category and the campus zone and building where it was lost, and follow their reports from an inbox that shows each one as Searching, Matches found, or Ready for pickup. Staff log found items and manage the inventory. When a report has likely matches, the member reviews them with limited details (name, category, building, and date found) and claims the one that is theirs, which holds it at that building until staff verify the owner's ID at pickup; members can also cancel reports they no longer need, and a campus map shows where the lost and found desks are. This evaluation focuses on the main features of the value proposition: reporting a lost item, understanding a report's status in the inbox, recognizing and claiming the right match from limited details, and, for staff, logging a found item and completing a pickup.
