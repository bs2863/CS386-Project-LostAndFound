### Search by Location

A **Campus** User can **_search by_** a Location to find lost or found items in a specific area on campus. Each **Location** has a _name_ and _description_ to identify the building or area where an item was lost or found. The system shows matching **Lost Item Reports** and **Found Item Report**s based on the location entered, making it easier for users to find their missing items.
