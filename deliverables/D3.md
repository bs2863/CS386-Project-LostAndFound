### Log Found Item (Brennan)

The system allows a **Staff Member** to log a **Found Item** so that it can later be matched against reports from people who lost something. A **Staff Member** **_logs_** each Found Item after recieving or discovering it. Each **Found Item** has a *description*, a *date_found*, a *location_found*, a *status* (such as unclaimed or matched), and a *holding_location* indictating where the item is currently kept. A found item may also **_include_** zero or more **Photos**, each of which has an *image_file*. The staff member submits this information through a **Found Item Report**, which **_records_** exactly one **Found Item**. The system **_validates_** the report before it is accepted.

### View Map of Items

A **Community Member** can view a map of campus, showing nearby **Lost Item**s matching a given _description_ and _location_. Most **Item**s are **_located in_** specific **Building**s, which also causes those **Building**s' _front desk location_ to be displayed.
