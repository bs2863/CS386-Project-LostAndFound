# 1. Positioning

### 1.1 Problem Statement

The problem of an inefficient campus lost and found system affects NAU students and faculty members who lose personal or academic items all over campus, forcing them to waste time physically searching for their lost items or outright giving them up.

### 1.2 Product position Statement

|  |  |
|--|--|
| For | NAU students and faculty |
|Who|need an organized way to connect lost items with the people who are looking for them|
|The NAU Lost & Found System|is a web based lost and found management app|
|That|allows people all over campus to efficiently search for and report lost belongings|
|Unlike|a traditional lost and found, which generally requires students to visit multiple locations or contact staff individually|
|Our product|will provide a standardized system for reporting and searching for lost belongings.|

### 1.3 Value Proposition and Customer Segment

Value Proposition: The NAU Lost and Found System is a web app that allows NAU students and faculty members to report and search for lost items or belongings, reducing the time and effort required to find said items. 

Consumer Segment: NAU students who have lost personal belongings on NAU campus.

# 3. Non-Functional Requirements
1.Usability: The system should be easy for students to use when reporting a lost or found item.
Measureable acceptance criterion: At least 90% of test users should be able to submit a lost or found item report in 5 minutes or less.


2. Performance Efficiency: The system should return search results quickly when a student searches for a lost item.
Measureable acceptance criterion: Search results should appear within 10 seconds for at least 95% of searches during testing.


3. Reliability: The system should work correctly during normal use without frequently failing.
Measureable acceptance criterion: At least 99% of user requests should be completed successfully during a 24-hour period.


4. Security: The system should keep the user's information protected from people who are not allowed to access it.
Measureable acceptance criterion: A user that is not an admin should not be able to change item descriptions or information.


5. Maintainability: The system should be easy for developers to update or fix without breaking other parts of the system.
Measureable acceptance criterion: After making a change to one part of the system, at least 95% of the existing program should still pass.

### Use Case: Log a Found Item into the System
**Actor:** Campus Staff Member

**Trigger:** A staff member receives or discovers a lost item and decides to log it into the Lost & Found system.

**Pre-conditions:** 
- The staff member has an active account and is logged into the system.
- The staff member is in possession of the found item or knows the location of said item.

**Post-conditions:** 
- The found item is registered with a short description, location, timestamp, and photo.
- The item is available for matching against existing or future lost item reports.

**Success Scenario:**

1. The staff member logs into the system and chooses to log a found item.
2. The system requests information about the found item.
3. The staff member provides a short description of the item.
4. The staff member specifies when and where the item was found.
5. The staff member uploads one or more photos of the item.
6. The staff member submits the entry.
7. The system validates the submission.
8. The system compares the entry against existing lost-item reports and flags any potential matches.
9. If a potential match is found, the system notifies the person/persons who reported the matching lost item for verification.
10. The system displays a confirmation to the staff member that the item was successfully logged.

**Alternate Scenarios:**
**9A. Duplicate Entry Detected**
1. The system flags the potential duplicate for staff review.
2. The staff member confirms whether to merge, submit separately, or cancel the new entry.

**9B. No existing match found**
1. The item is stored as unclaimed and remains available for future matching.
2. No notification is sent.
